I tried to make the lab more realistic by mimicing a real-world business that sells its RAG Application, AskKontor. The story follows..

## Introducing AskKontor

A small AI Startup decides to sell its chatbot named AskKontor to its customers. The problem they want to solve - retraining a model on company data demands a lot of resources. Therefore, their chatbot allows users to upload/feed their documents to AskKontor, and then have AskKontor answer company-specific questions.

The company wanted to be one of the first to the market, so, they shipped fast (ofcourse by cutting corners! ;) ).

### Customers

Right now, the company has just two customers. So, there are two tenants. Let's list them below along with their employees and their relevant access rights on their docs.

**The Hopfenstein Brewery** is just a regular craft brewery with a "secret" beer recipe.

| Person | Role          | Access                                           |
|--------|---------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Anna   | HR lead       | Salary list (confidential), handbook             |
| Ben    | Head brewer   | The secret "Saar Dunkel" recipe, handbook        |

**Weber & Klein** is a law firm.

| Person | Role      | Access                                                             |
|--------|-----------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Lena   | Partner   | Privileged client file, settlement draft (+ its private appendix), firm wiki |
| Tom    | Paralegal | Settlement draft (main part only), firm wiki                       |

That is it.

Additionally, we are the external pentest partner of the startup. We only have the rights to upload. In the access control matrix, we aren't supposed to have any other access.

With that said, let's get started with getting a basic idea of the architecture and infrastructure components of the lab.


#Infrastructure Components

## Backend = FastAPI
Fast and quick. Nothing fancy. I made it handle the login, chat with the AI, upload, and the vulnerable logic.

## Vector Store = Qdrant
This is the vector database for our startup. This holds the chunk embeddings and payload tags (tenant, doc_id, and classification).

## Database = PostgresQL
This is where we store our facts. The documents, the ACL details.

## Chat and Embeddings Model

I am using OpenRouter to have access to all the latest AI models via one single API interface.
For embeddings, I am using `text-embedding-3-small`. 
For answering and chatting, `gpt-4o-mini`

These are cheap and perfect for our little lab.
